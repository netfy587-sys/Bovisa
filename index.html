Why It Matters

Why Games Matter

People have played games for thousands of years. Races, ball games and board games appear in the oldest cities, and they still bring friends, families and whole countries together today.

Every game teaches something about patience, rules and respect.

Rules make play fair

A game only works when everyone agrees on the rules. Learning to follow them teaches fairness, and learning to question them gently is how new games are invented.

Practice builds skill

Nobody is great at the start. Small, regular practice slowly turns clumsy movements into smooth ones, which is a lesson that helps in studies and work as well.

Teams share the effort

In team games each player has a role. Winning depends on listening, passing the ball and trusting others, and losing teaches how to try again with a smile.

Minds play too

Puzzles and board games train memory and planning. Try learning one new game this month and teach it to a friend.
